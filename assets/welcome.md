# A little room to think.

A quiet place for your words. No crowded toolbars, no preview pane. Just you, a thought, and somewhere to take it.

## Find your flow

Writing feels better when the tools get out of the way. **Daytrip** brings your Markdown to life in the same space you write it. Type Markdown and watch its syntax fall away. Move, select, and keep writing across the whole page.

> Almost all good writing begins with terrible first efforts. You need to start somewhere.
>
> — Anne Lamott

## Make something of it

- Start with a small idea. Give it a heading.
- Add **a little emphasis**, or a link to [somewhere inspiring](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/).
- Take your time. There is no wrong first sentence.
