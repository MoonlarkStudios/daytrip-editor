# The quiet hour

*A small field note on making room to notice.*

At the edge of the morning, the coast is almost colourless. Water folds around the rocks. A path disappears into the grass. For a few minutes, there is nothing to answer and nowhere else to be.

We came here to write about attention. We stayed because the light kept changing.
