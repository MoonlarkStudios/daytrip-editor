# Community conduct

Koan welcomes contributions from people of every background and experience
level. Be considerate, explain your reasoning, and keep technical disagreement
focused on the work. Give others room to ask questions and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Do not use project discussions to advertise unrelated products or services.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, code reviews and other project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent;
maintainers are accountable to the same expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to [John Slaughter](mailto:john@moonlarkstudios.com).
Include the relevant links and context. Reports will be handled discreetly, with
information shared only as needed to address the concern. Security vulnerabilities
have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
